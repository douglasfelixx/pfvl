1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

ORBE SISTEMAS INTEGRADOS DE GESTAO FINANCEIRA LTDA (Orbefin Sistemas Financeiros), registered with Brazil’s CNPJ under number 69.815.402/0001-40, is responsible for processing the personal data described in this Policy. For questions or requests about your data, email privacidade@orbefin.com.br. You may use this channel without signing in.

This Policy covers the PFVL website, including its content, community spaces, and support channels, and the PFVL application used for financial organization and planning. The data processed depends on the service and features you use.

2. Data we process and why

  • Account and profile. We use account information, such as your display name, verified email address, and account identifier, to create and manage your access, verify your identity, maintain your session, and save preferences. We also use your email for account verification or recovery when those flows are available.
  • Website use. We process posts, comments, replies, votes, reviews, suggestions, notifications, and related media to operate community spaces, show your history, apply community rules, and send notices within the portal.
  • Support. We use contact details, messages, and request history to record, track, and respond to requests. Send only information needed for support. Do not send passwords, access codes, bank details, or documents that have not been requested.
  • Financial application. When you use the application, we process information you enter, such as income, expenses, accounts, commitments, and goals, to organize records, calculate amounts, and create projections. If a financial connection is available and you enable it, the data received and its intended use will be explained during the connection-authorization step.
  • Subscriptions and billing. When you make a purchase, we process account details and information about the purchase and payment to manage the plan, provide support, process payments or refunds, and meet applicable obligations.
  • Operation and security. We may process technical access and browsing data, such as access logs and device or browser information, to keep the service available, diagnose issues, protect accounts, and prevent abuse.

3. Legal bases for processing

We use data needed to provide a contracted service or handle steps you request before entering into a contract. When a legal or regulatory obligation applies, we process the data needed to meet it. To protect accounts and keep the service working, we may rely on legitimate interests where applicable and where the person’s rights are respected. We may also process data to exercise rights in administrative, court, or arbitration proceedings. When a purpose requires consent, we will ask for specific authorization, which you may withdraw through the channel or control provided.

4. What may be public

Posts, comments, replies, and reviews shown in public areas may appear with your display name and the media you associate with the contribution, as indicated in the interface. People without accounts may also see published material. Keep personal or financial information you want to protect out of public spaces.

Support requests, suggestions sent as private messages, and team replies are available to your account and authorized team members. When a suggestion leads to a published improvement, the public area uses a separate title and description that do not identify the person or reproduce the private message. Site search shows public results and, for signed-in users, private records that their account is allowed to access.

5. Who may receive data

We may share data needed with providers that support functions such as user identification, service operation, storage, communications, and payment processing, depending on the feature used. Financial connections involve the relevant institutions and integration services and follow the information and permissions shown during connection. We may also provide data to authorities or other recipients when required by law or needed to exercise legal rights.

If personal data is transferred to recipients outside Brazil, the transfer will follow the mechanisms provided by data-protection law.

6. Cookies and preferences

The website uses cookies and other browser features to provide access and security, remember preferences, and record your privacy choices. Use “Cookie preferences” in the footer to review available options. You may also clear data through your browser. This may end your session or remove saved preferences. If optional features require consent, they will be presented separately so you can choose and change your decision.

7. How long we retain data

We retain each type of data for as long as needed for the purpose described in this Policy. We consider how long our relationship with you lasts, the status of support requests, legal obligations, and the need to exercise rights. If records need to be kept after an account closes, access will be restricted and use will be limited to that purpose.

Not using the service, subscription expiration, and suspension do not automatically delete contributions. Suggestions and support requests remain private and follow their own retention criteria. You may use support and privacy channels even without account access.

After a portal account is permanently deleted, we keep a public contribution only if it no longer identifies you. This includes your name, profile image, the content itself, media, and links. If it could still identify you, we remove the content from public areas.

For the financial application, the Terms of Use provide one year of read-only access after a subscription expires and deletion of the application account at the end of that period if it is not renewed. Deleted data may remain in protected backups for up to 30 days, as described in the Terms. The one-year period applies to the financial application.

8. How we protect data

We use technical and administrative safeguards appropriate to the processing, including restricting access to people who need the information to perform their work. Report suspected unauthorized access through official channels. Keep your credentials and exported files in a secure place.

9. Your rights and how to make a request

You may request confirmation of processing, access to data, and corrections. Depending on the circumstances, you may also request anonymization, blocking or deletion, data portability, information about sharing, withdrawal of consent and deletion of data processed on that basis, subject to legal retention requirements. You may object to processing that does not comply with the law and request a review of decisions based solely on automated processing that affect your interests. You may also ask whether you can refuse consent and what the consequences would be.

Send your request to privacidade@orbefin.com.br. We may ask for only the information strictly needed to verify your identity and protect your data. We will respond under the applicable deadlines and conditions. You may also submit a petition to Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), as provided by law.

10. Changes to this Policy

The current version and update date will be available on this page. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels. If a new purpose requires consent, we will request consent specifically for that purpose.
