---
name: PFVL public roadmap status identity
description: Stable stage keys and historical references for the public suggestions roadmap.
---

Public roadmap stages have a stable identity separate from their Portuguese and English labels, order, and active state. Keep inactive stages available for displaying existing suggestions, but exclude them from new selections. Keep public roadmap stages separate from private suggestion statuses and from support-request statuses.

**Why:** Renaming or deactivating a stage must not silently rewrite an existing suggestion's history, and public progress must not change the private status shown to its requester.

**How to apply:** Change stage labels, ordering, or availability without changing the stable key or reassigning existing suggestions. Preserve historical stage labels when rendering published roadmap cards.
